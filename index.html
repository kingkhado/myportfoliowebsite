My Testimony:
From Darkness to Light

Scroll down to experience my journey...

I had a bad trip one day... saw demons and devils.

I prayed so hard... then I saw Jesus.

Woke up the next day thinking it was just a bad trip.

Continued living an evil lifestyle.

Drove back home one day from a life of sin.

Watched a testimony video — everything sounded just like me.

Gave my life to Christ.

Started being aware of the spiritual realm.

After transitioning to Christ's Kingdom, I realized I made myself a target to the kingdom of darkness — spiritual warfare began.

But greater is He that is in me,
than he that is in the world.

— 1 John 4:4
